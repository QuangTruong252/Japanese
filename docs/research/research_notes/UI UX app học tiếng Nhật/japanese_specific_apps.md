# UI/UX of Japanese-specific learning apps and textbook sites (illustration-rich, story-driven, Vietnamese-market)

Research date: 2026-10-07. ~19 tool calls. App Store version dates below have no year in the source; they were fetched on 2026-10-07, so they are most likely Aug–Oct 2026. NHK WORLD (www3.nhk.or.jp) blocked fetching; Irodori "how-to" PDF could not be text-extracted. Several UI questions (exact home-screen layout) could not be verified from text sources and are listed under Gaps rather than guessed.

## Q1. What is on the home screen / first viewport, and how is "what to study today" shown?

### Takeaway
The Vietnamese commercial apps (Mochi, Migii, Todaii) surface "today" through mechanisms rather than a lesson map: a review-timing signal (Mochi "Golden Time"), a personalized roadmap with daily lessons (Migii), or a daily-updated news feed (Todaii). Streaks, widgets, leaderboards and reminders were added recently. Japan Foundation's Minato instead opens on a filterable course catalog (level / skill / explanation language), with no daily-task framing.

### Cited Findings
- MochiKanji: "BRIGHT & FRIENDLY DESIGN" with positive, energetic aesthetics; mascot duo Mochi & Michi "accompany learners throughout the process"; "GOLDEN TIME" uses learning history to remind users to review "at the moment right before you forget". — [App Store VN, MochiKanji](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/mochikanji-learn-japanese/id1463353686)
- MochiKanji recent versions add engagement surfaces: 5.10.0 (4 Sept) streaks and new widgets; 5.11.0 (8 Sept) leaderboard; rating 4.5/5 from 4.4k ratings. — [App Store VN, MochiKanji](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/mochikanji-learn-japanese/id1463353686)
- MochiKanji is described as "bright colors to bring a positive, energetic feeling" with the "cute duo called Mochi & Michi". — [App Store US, MochiKanji](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mochikanji-learn-japanese/id1463353686?uo=4)
- MochiKanji web page: "structured learning path, featuring bite-sized lessons", spaced repetition, "6000+ Japanese words and Kanjis" (App Store listing says 8000+; the figures differ between sources). — [kanji.mochidemy.com](https://kanji.mochidemy.com/learn-japanese); [App Store VN](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/mochikanji-learn-japanese/id1463353686)
- Migii JLPT: "customized roadmaps based on your unique strengths and weaknesses with daily lessons to keep you on track"; daily reminders and progress tracking; integrated Mazii dictionary (voice, camera, handwriting). — [All Language Resources / aggregator summary](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/mytest-migii/); [appfollow listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/migii-jlpt-jlpt-test-n5-n1/1463267540?country=td)
- Migii JLPT VN listing: skill-separated sections (kanji, grammar, listening, reading), continuously updated mock exams, online competitions with leaderboards/prizes, certificate generation, weakness identification, daily reminders, study history. Version 6.2.0–6.2.3 shipped Sept–Oct (fetched 2026-10-07). — [App Store VN, Migii JLPT](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/migii-jlpt-jlpt-test-n5-n1/id1463267540?l=vi)
- Migii exam sections are organized by skill: vocabulary (kanji reading, orthography, contextual expressions) and grammar (sentence composition); 40 mock exams with improvement recommendations at the end. — [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/mytest-migii/)
- Todaii: reading practice through N5–N1 news articles, AI pronunciation scoring, flashcards, JLPT mock tests, listening with transcripts, conversation practice, integrated dictionary lookup, "Tomo Chat" AI speaking; rating 4.8/5 (2.8k); v4.4.9 (18 Sept); includes third-party ads. — [App Store VN, Todaii](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/todaii-learn-japanese-kanji/id1107177166)
- Todaii content: 22+ topics (news, culture, entertainment) sourced from NHK, CNN, BBC, revised daily per level N5–N1; "1-touch lookup" of words and whole sentences without leaving the app. — [benable / summary](https://benable.com/leveluplyle/39b748338bf4b45f87d0) (aggregator, secondary)
- Minato (Japan Foundation): home page has Sign up / Login / User Guide and interface language selector incl. Vietnamese; ~95 courses as cards showing level (A1–B2), explanation language, free/charged, application status; filters by level, category (integrated skills, reading, writing, listening, speaking, vocabulary, grammar, culture) and explanation language; Self-Study vs Tutor Support; "Community" tab; links out to IRODORI and MARUGOTO Plus. — [minato-jf.jp](https://www.minato-jf.jp)
- Ringotan (kanji writing): SRS + handwriting input detection; recent options to limit new kanji per day and to put new kanji before reviews; no ads/paywalls; 4.9/5 from 538 reviews. — [App Store, Ringotan](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1633001064); [WaniKani community thread](https://community.wanikani.com/t/ringotan-free-app-for-learning-how-to-write-kanji/51702)

### Inferences
- The "today" signal in VN apps is a review/roadmap queue plus gamification (streak, leaderboard, widget), not a textbook-chapter position. For a Minna-based app, the closest analog is "next lesson in the book + reviews due", which none of the generic apps express as a textbook position.
- Ringotan's "new before reviews" and "daily new cap" options suggest power users want control over the ordering of the daily queue.
- Minato's catalog-first home is appropriate for a multi-course platform but has no daily-study framing; it is a counter-example for a single-textbook personal app.

### Gaps
- No verified screenshot-level description of Mochi, Migii or Todaii home screens (card order, mascot placement, where Golden Time appears). App Store text does not describe layout; screenshots were not readable via text fetch.
- Kanji Study (Chase Colburn) and Dekiru/HeyJapan/Riki were not researched within the call budget.

## Q2. How do textbook-based products present a lesson as a scene/situation with characters, and connect illustration to language?

### Takeaway
Japan Foundation (Irodori, Marugoto) and NHK Easy Japanese are scene-first: each lesson is a real-life situation or story episode (topic/Can-do), introduced through audio drama/skit plus illustrations, then broken into a key phrase, grammar and culture. NHK uses a persistent cast (Anna and friends) across 48 episodes; Irodori/Marugoto organize by everyday-life topic and Can-do goal. Minna no Nihongo companions in the VN market are mostly third-party (JMina, iMina), structured as vocab/grammar/kanji/listening lists rather than scenes.

### Cited Findings
- NHK Easy Japanese: offered in 18 languages; "lessons designed in audio-drama style"; heroine Anna, a 20-year-old Thai student who loves manga, studying one year at a Tokyo university; experiences classes, dorm life, shopping, travel; 48-part series of skits forming an extended story, with tourist info and culture. — [mylittledejima.com (2017)](https://mylittledejima.com/2017/05/26/nhk-world-easy-japanese/) (older source, 2015 series)
- NHK supporting cast: Sakura (tutor/friend), Kenta (Sakura's cousin), Rodrigo (classmate from Mexico), Prof. Suzuki, Dorm Mother. — [mylittledejima.com](https://mylittledejima.com/2017/05/26/nhk-world-easy-japanese/)
- NHK lesson structure: each ~10-min episode starts with a short story focused on "one key sentence/expressions"; explanations of words, tenses, particles, verb forms; segment "Teach Us, Teacher" where the supervisor explains learning points; "Sound Words" segment on onomatopoeia; PDF textbook has "colorful illustrations of the skits". 48 ten-minute audio lessons + 48 thirty-second video lessons. — [mylittledejima.com](https://mylittledejima.com/2017/05/26/nhk-world-easy-japanese/); [mylittledejima category page](https://mylittledejima.com/category/fun-with-comic-essays/)
- Irodori: organized by topic; "each lesson deals with a different topic connected to everyday life in Japan"; emphasizes listening input with plenty of audio; free PDFs downloadable worldwide for tablet/phone or print. Elementary 1 & 2 released March 2020, Starter November 2020. — [Japan Foundation news 2021-05](https://www.jpf.go.jp/e/project/japanese/teach/tsushin/news/202105.html)
- Irodori levels: Starter (A1), Elementary 1 (A2), Elementary 2 (A2), Pre-Intermediate (A2/B1); 18 lessons each; MP3 audio, answer keys, word lists, grammar worksheets and illustration lists downloadable; Can-do objective: "increase what you can do using Japanese in real-life situations", based on "JF Can-do for Life in Japan" (2019). — [irodori.jpf.go.jp/about](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/about.html)
- Irodori Pre-Intermediate (A2/B1) cover revealed 2024. — [JF New Delhi news](https://nd.jpf.go.jp/?p=17087)
- Tofugu on Irodori: "The content and design of the books are very high quality, and it's hard to believe that they're free!"; organized around practical topics (e.g., employment in Japan). — [Tofugu resource database](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-learning-resources-database/irodori-japanese-for-life-in-japan/) (2020-era entry)
- Marugoto: Marugoto Japanese Online Course runs on Minato (Self-Study and Tutor Support, A1 to A2/B1); MARUGOTO Plus follows Marugoto topics with kanji, grammar and "conversations while watching videos" (A1–A2); "MARUGOTO Words" dictionary with audio. — [marugoto.jpf.go.jp e-learning](https://marugoto.jpf.go.jp/en/e-learning/)
- Minato launched 2016 by JF Japanese-Language Institute, Kansai; main course is Marugoto online. — [JF press release 2016](https://www.jpf.go.jp/e/about/press/2016/022.html) (older)
- Minna no Nihongo companions: "Học tiếng Nhật Minna A-Z JMina" covers Minna I and II with vocab, grammar, kanji, reading, listening, flashcards, tests, lookup; iMina claims 512 kanji with illustrations and radicals, video lectures, JLPT questions; 3A Network provides an official audio app for Shokyu I 3rd edition (sentence patterns, example sentences, conversations, exercises). An "official" Minna app exists for Chinese learners (FLTRP with Haidi). — [appshunter JMina](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/minna-nihongo-a/id1510762658); [iphoneaddict iMina](https://iphoneaddict.fr/apps/education/learn-minnano-nihongo-imina.html); [3A Network audio](https://www.3anet.co.jp/np/resrcs/140021/); [mergeek](https://mergeek.com/en/latest/yEQ0bn1ax3Kndwjz)
- WaniKani (Tofugu) radical illustrations, announced 2025-01-14: artist Aya plus content team publish "a handful of images each week" starting at Level 1; style matches Tofugu's hiragana/katakana guides; goal is to connect shape to name at a glance so learners don't make an "extra leap" (example: dropping the "private"→"poop" step); some abstract radicals may never get art. — [WaniKani blog](https://blog.wanikani.com/posts/illustrations-for-radicals/); [WaniKani Knowledge Base](https://knowledge.wanikani.com/wanikani/radical-images/)
- WaniKani community earlier tried AI (DALL-E 3 / anime-style) mnemonic images via userscripts. — [WaniKani community](https://community.wanikani.com/t/were-using-dall-e-3-to-generate-mnemonic-images-for-every-item-on-wk/63431); [GitHub userscript](https://github.com/chikorita157/WaniKani-AI-Anime-Mnemonic-Images)

### Inferences
- Pattern across JF/NHK: Situation (illustrated scene / skit audio) → one key phrase → explanation → practice → culture note. The illustration carries the situation so the text can stay short; a persistent cast (NHK) builds familiarity over 48 episodes.
- Minna no Nihongo itself has fixed recurring characters (Mike Miller, etc.) and per-lesson conversation scenes, but none of the found VN companion apps appear to exploit them as scene-first entry; they are list/flashcard-centric. This is an open niche for a Minna-based personal app (an inference; not verified by screenshots).
- WaniKani's choice of a single human illustrator with a consistent style, released gradually and skipping abstract items, is a realistic model: illustrate only where shape→meaning is concrete.

### Gaps
- Could not verify Irodori/Marugoto page layouts (whether dialogues appear in speech bubbles, illustration placement, character names) — the how-to PDF was binary and the About page doesn't describe it.
- NHK WORLD's current Easy Japanese site (2023–2026 redesign, newer series, quiz pages) could not be fetched; findings come from a 2017 blog describing the 2015 series.
- Hirogaru (JF site about Japanese culture topics) not researched.

## Q3. How do they render Japanese text (furigana toggle, font, size)?

### Takeaway
Little verifiable detail was found. NHK Easy Japanese materials show romaji alongside kana/kanji; Todaii's selling point is tap-to-lookup on any word/sentence in news text. No source confirmed furigana-toggle or font specifics for Mochi, Migii, Irodori or Marugoto.

### Cited Findings
- NHK Easy Japanese materials present "Romaji ... as well as Japanese characters with Hiragana, Katakana and Kanji". — [mylittledejima.com](https://mylittledejima.com/2017/05/26/nhk-world-easy-japanese/)
- Todaii: "1-touch lookup" for words and whole sentences inside articles. — [benable summary](https://benable.com/leveluplyle/39b748338bf4b45f87d0)
- Migii: listening practice with subtitles; integrated dictionary. — [App Store VN, Migii](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/migii-jlpt-jlpt-test-n5-n1/id1463267540?l=vi)
- Ringotan's focus is handwriting input (stroke detection), i.e., kanji rendered as a writing canvas, not just display. — [App Store, Ringotan](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1633001064)

### Inferences
- In beginner-facing JF/NHK material, scaffolding is layered (romaji/kana/kanji together); reading-oriented apps (Todaii) rely on tap-to-reveal rather than always-on furigana.

### Gaps
- Furigana toggle behavior, Japanese font choice and type size in Mochi/Migii/Todaii/Irodori/Marugoto: no text source found. Needs hands-on screenshots.
- Whether Irodori prints furigana over all kanji or only first occurrences: not verified.

## Q4. What do Vietnamese users praise/criticize about Mochi, Migii, Todaii UI?

### Takeaway
Praise centers on cute/bright branding and vivid images+sound (Mochi), completeness of exam question types (Migii), and an "intuitive, friendly interface" (Todaii). Complaints are about stability (Todaii crashes, Migii rotation UI bug), level-inappropriate content and TTS audio (Migii), and ads/tracking (Todaii).

### Cited Findings
- MochiKanji VN reviews: "Gây nghiện" (addictive) praising "hình ảnh âm thanh sinh động" (vivid images and sound) and reminder notifications; "App hay" praising cute branding and scientifically organized vocabulary; a user asking for specialized (medical) vocabulary and willing to pay. — [App Store VN, MochiKanji](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/mochikanji-learn-japanese/id1463353686)
- Migii VN reviews: "Đầy đủ dạng bài, không như các app khác" (all question types, unlike other apps); one "lỗi giao diện" (UI bug) when rotating on iPhone X, developer says fixed; requests for more N1 content. Rating shown 4.7/5; the fetch reported "5.9 million reviews", which is implausible and likely a parsing error (another source reports 4.5 from 497 ratings on a different storefront). — [App Store VN, Migii](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/migii-jlpt-jlpt-test-n5-n1/id1463267540?l=vi); [appfollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/migii-jlpt-jlpt-test-n5-n1/1463267540?country=td)
- Migii criticism (English review site): some bugs, some tests include vocab/grammar above the chosen level, listening uses text-to-speech rather than native speakers. — [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/mytest-migii/)
- Todaii VN reviews: "Giao diện trực quan thân thiện" (intuitive friendly interface), "good content"; criticism "Hay lỗi thoát ứng dụng" (frequent crashes) and listening/pronunciation section not working on a device; update fixed iPad interface issues; app tracks identifiers, collects user content and audio, includes third-party ads. — [App Store VN, Todaii](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/todaii-learn-japanese-kanji/id1107177166)

### Inferences
- For Vietnamese learners, "friendly" is associated with bright color, mascots, images and sound per word (Mochi) and a clean reading interface (Todaii); trust is eroded by crashes, ads and mismatched-level content more than by visual style.
- Mochi's addition of streaks/leaderboards/widgets in 2026 suggests the VN market rewards engagement mechanics; a personal, calm app can deliberately skip these.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese blog or Facebook-group reviews were retrieved (search returned only store/aggregator pages); sentiment above is from a small set of App Store reviews and is not representative.
- Google Play reviews (likely larger VN volume) not fetched.
